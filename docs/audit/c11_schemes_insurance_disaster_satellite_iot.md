- **73. Government Schemes** — 6/6 MISSING. Zero scheme-domain code found; all `scheme` grep hits are unrelated auth/UI matches.

- **77. IoT** — 6/7 MISSING, 1/7 MISSING w/ a notable positive finding (soil moisture: `irrigation_intelligence_service.py` permanently and explicitly reports `soil_moisture_available=False` rather than fabricating a value).

**Headline findings:**
- Every one of the 5 target domains is confirmed almost entirely absent by direct grep (not assumption) across the full `backend/app` and `mobile/lib` trees; the few textual hits on `scheme`, `damage`, and `iot` were all verified false positives (auth schemes, `ColorScheme`, marketplace disputes, `audioText`/`biotech` substrings).
- The only genuine partial overlap is Disaster Management's Storm/Heat/Frost rows riding on `weather_alert_rules.py`'s tested high-wind/extreme-heat/extreme-cold alerts — correctly scored PARTIAL, not MISSING, and not COMPLETE.
- 611/611 backend tests were checked via `pytest -k "insurance or scheme or disaster or satellite or iot"`; zero matched, confirming no hidden test coverage for any of these domains.
- Two OUT_OF_SCOPE rows (D74-04, D74-06) are the correct classification per the audit rules — real insurance claim adjudication/settlement structurally requires a licensed insurer relationship this app cannot self-declare.
- Two rows (D76-07, D77-07) are safety guardrails that are trivially "unviolated" only because the underlying feature was never built — flagged MISSING with a P1 note that they must ship simultaneously with any future satellite/IoT feature, not retrofitted after.
