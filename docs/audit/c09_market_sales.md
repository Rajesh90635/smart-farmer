# C09 Audit: Market Prices, Comparison, Net Realization, Buyer Matching, eNAM/FPO Boundaries, Sales & Orders (Domains 56-63)

This audit covers domains 56-63 of the external checklist against the actual code in
`C:\FARMER_APP\smart-farmer-offline-sync-verified\smart-farmer`. The single biggest structural fact governing
domains 56-59 is that **this app has no real crop-selling market-price data source at all**: `backend/app/api/v1/market.py`
is an explicitly empty reserved router ("No endpoints yet"), and `docs/PRICE_DATA_SOURCES.md` states plainly "No live
external price feed is integrated," naming exactly what a real Agmarknet/eNAM integration would require rather than
faking one. The only real price infrastructure in the codebase (`ReferencePrice`, `dealer_price_history`,
`price_anomaly_flag`, Scam Shield) is scoped exclusively to **input products farmers buy** (seed/fertilizer/pesticide),
confirmed by `ReferencePrice.product_id` FK to `products` and by Phase 32's own inspection note in
`PROJECT_STATUS.md` line 516 ("`ReferencePrice`... is scoped entirely to Prompt 9's agricultural **input**
products... It has nothing to do with crop selling prices"). Crop selling itself is powered entirely by
`HarvestListing` (a farmer's own asking price) + `BuyerOffer`/`CounterOffer` (private bilateral negotiation) +
`SaleOrder` (an 11-state fulfillment lifecycle) — real, tested, and honest, but structurally incapable of mandi
price discovery, multi-market comparison, or true net-realization math (the `charges` field is hardcoded to zero at
every sale, so `net_value` can never differ from `gross_value`). No fabricated eNAM integration, fake live-bidding,
or invented FPO legal structure was found anywhere — domains 60 and 61 are honestly absent, exactly as expected.
Domains 62 (crop-sale lifecycle) and 63 (dealer input-order lifecycle) are the most mature areas audited, with 42/42
targeted backend tests passing and broad state-machine coverage.

Tests were run and passed: `cd backend; pytest tests/test_marketplace_offers.py tests/test_orders.py
tests/test_price_comparison.py -q` → **42 passed** (confirmed live in this session).
